Pricing FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Does the plan auto-renew?

No. Weekly Pro is one-time. When the 7 days end, you're back to free. Renew whenever you need.

Can I get a partial refund?

Because activation is manual and instant, we don't do partial refunds. If your account can't generate due to our error, we credit extra days.

What happens if I renew before expiry?

The new 7 days are added on top of your current expiry — no days lost.

Are there discounts for volume?

Talk to us via a support ticket. If you have a genuine high-volume use case we can work something out.
