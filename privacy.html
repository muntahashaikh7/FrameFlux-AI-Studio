Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. What we collect

Account information (name, email), the prompts and images you submit for generation, payment references and screenshots you upload for verification, and standard technical logs (IP, browser).

2. How we use it

To run your account, process your generations, verify payments, prevent abuse, and provide support. We do not sell your data.

3. Third parties

Prompts and (if provided) source images are sent to our underlying model providers to produce your generations. Payment screenshots are stored only for verification.

4. Retention

We keep your generations in your gallery until you delete them or close your account. Payment records are retained for accounting purposes.

5. Your rights

Email info@veoframe.com to access, correct or delete your data.
