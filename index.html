Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Weekly Pro pricing plan work?

It costs exactly Rs 499 for 7 days. It is completely non-recurring: your plan simply expires at the end of the duration, and you decide if you wish to purchase it again. No surprise charges.

How do automated payments work?

When you click Subscribe, you can choose PayFast (supporting Visa, Mastercard, JazzCash wallet, EasyPaisa wallet, and major Pakistani bank accounts) or Binance Pay. The system will handle the checkout and activate your account instantly after a successful payment.

Can I sign up and preview the studio first?

Yes! Creating an account is completely free. You can browse the model directories, explore prompts, and inspect your gallery workspace before purchasing a subscription.

What happens if my generation job fails?

We monitor API status. If any video or image generation fails, the system automatically refunds the quota unit back to your daily limit immediately.

Do I get support if something goes wrong?

Absolutely. You can open a ticket inside your support dashboard. We monitor tickets actively and provide human replies to billing or generation inquiries.
